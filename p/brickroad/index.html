Raise Readiness & Commercial Architecture

For Freeman Lewin

Executive Summary

Brickroad's product is ahead of its narrative, with eight of the top ten target funds already in trial. The raise opens in September and closes near Thanksgiving, which sets the clock for everything preceding it.

Three structural conditions limit the business in the meantime: trial conversion cycles run closer to six months than six weeks, sales and operations responsibilities sit entirely with the founder, and the investor and buyer narrative remains underbuilt against a defined raise window.

Diagnostic work from an initial engagement identified four gaps that resolve into a single commercial function: sequencing which buyer vertical follows hedge funds, a single narrative spine serving both buyer and investor audiences, a closing motion that differs by buyer type, and retention ownership with no current owner.

This engagement prepares and runs the raise, builds the commercial function alongside it, and continues as the operator of that function once the round closes.

The Build

Two phases. Phase One prepares and runs the raise while building the commercial function underneath it. Phase Two begins at its close and continues as an ongoing engagement.

  • Raise ReadinessPhase One · leads · August–November 2026

    Preparation and execution of the September raise, including the transition off founder-led sales.

    • A single investor narrative and deck, with figures held current across all investor communications
      • Pitch coaching through recurring working sessions, with narrative sequencing pressure-tested against real feedback
    • Investor-aligned milestones defined against existing relationships, including usage proxies that carry the narrative ahead of revenue
    • An investor target list, staged and scored, supported by list-triage tooling and advance exposure ahead of the raise opening
    • Meeting preparation, direct introductions with context, and follow-through to close
  • Commercial ArchitecturePhase One · follows · build-ready end of August

    The retention and conversion system designed ahead of the raise rather than improvised during it.

    • An evaluation of the current retention system, with success metrics defined for internal and client-facing use
    • Client-facing retention northstars for the current and following quarters
    • A closing motion documented by buyer type, with the buyer vertical sequence that follows hedge funds
    • Tactical processes and playbooks for deployment as the client base scales
  • Head of CommercialPhase Two · December 2026 onward

    Ownership of the commercial function as the business deploys the round, beginning automatically at the close of Phase One.

    • Ownership of the retention function against the northstars established in Phase One
    • Conversion cycle compression, measured against the current six-month baseline
    • Buyer vertical expansion, sequenced and executed
    • Commercial reporting on a standing cadence, with a quarterly review of function performance and scope

How It Would Work

Phase One: $7,000 per month, August through November 2026. Invoiced monthly in advance on the first of the month.

Phase Two: $13,500 per month, beginning December 2026. Continues month to month until either party provides thirty days' written notice.

Requirements: resolution of two open scoping questions before August 1 — whether all trials are currently free, and whether smaller, buyable products exist that could compress the six-month cycle.

Beyond the Build

The commercial function continues as the business scales. Two directions extend it.

  • Commercial Team Leadership
    Definition and hiring of the first commercial hires, with the function's playbooks already documented and running.
  • The Following Raise
    The narrative spine, investor relationships, and milestone framework built here carry directly into the next round.

Next Step

Confirmation of this scope will be followed by a detailed set of deliverables and a signed Statement of Work, with work beginning August 2026.
